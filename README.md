I built a robot arm in Blender from a cylinder and three cubes, then added an armature with four connected bones (Base, Shoulder, Elbow, Gripper) and parented each mesh to its bone with Ctrl+P > Bone. Finally, I exported it as an FBX with +Z forward and Y up. The easiest part was fixing the bone lengths: when I typed a wrong value while extruding, I just corrected it in the N panel. The hardest part was parenting, which failed at first because I had the wrong selection order and my mouse was not over the 3D viewport.
